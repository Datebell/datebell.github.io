How to get reminded before a free trial ends on iPhone

Short answer

Add the trial to Datebell with the date it ends. Datebell reminds you 2 days before, so there’s time to cancel. Apple suggests cancelling at least 24 hours before a trial ends, and many other services charge on that day too.

  1. Step 1

    Tap + and pick the service

    Open Datebell and tap + on Home. Under Subscriptions, search for the service or pick it from the list. Over 1,200 services come with their name and logo. Not there? Tap Add your own.

  2. Step 2

    Turn on Free trial and set the end date

    Turn on Free trial and choose the day the trial ends. Add the price you’ll pay after it, so the reminder tells you what’s at stake.

  3. Step 3

    Save. Datebell rings 2 days before

    The reminder comes 2 days before the trial ends, at your reminder time (9:00 AM unless you change it in Settings). Home shows how many days are left.

    DatebellnowFree trial ends in 2 daysYour Disney+ free trial ends in 2 days. Cancel at least a day before if you don’t want to pay.
  4. Step 4

    Cancel it, or keep it

    Open the trial to see when it ends. Tap Cancel or ask for a refund to see where to cancel: the App Store, Google Play, the service’s own website, or your phone provider. If you keep it, Datebell carries on as a normal subscription and reminds you before each payment.

Where to find the date a trial ends

For trials billed by Apple, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions (Apple’s guide). For trials you started on a website, check the welcome email or the account page on that site.

Can I do it without an app?

Yes. In the Reminders app, add a reminder for 2 days before the trial ends. Datebell does the counting for you, keeps the price and the way to cancel with it, and keeps reminding you before each payment if you stay.
